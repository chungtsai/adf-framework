# Issue Tracking Policy

## Principles

1. ADF artifacts are the source of truth for specification, rules, verification, and evidence.
2. External Issues represent committed work and workflow status.
3. Finding != Issue.
4. UNKNOWN != Issue.
5. REVIEW_REQUIRED != Issue.
6. External issue creation requires human confirmation unless project policy explicitly allows otherwise.
7. Closing an external Issue requires ADF completion gates to pass.

## Completion gates

- Required verification passed
- Traceability passed
- No blocking review items
- Human final approval completed
